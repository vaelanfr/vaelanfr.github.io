Areas of contribution

Making sustainable performance management operational

A performance management system is valuable when it changes how an organisation understands its situation, navigates competing objectives and makes decisions. VAELAN combines management tools, research-intervention and hands-on support to help teams embed sustainability into everyday practices.

Action research & organisational learning

Implementation & capability building

Make change usable and lasting

How VAELAN works

From diagnosis to real-world adoption

An adaptable research-intervention approach, shaped by each organisation’s context, existing practices and capacity to change. The stages below describe a working method, not a standardised promise of results.

01 · UnderstandStart with the organisation

Explore strategy, stakeholders, existing tools, management cycles and tensions between economic, social and environmental priorities.

02 · Co-designBuild with the people involved

Define useful questions, indicators, qualitative insights, responsibilities and decision-support tools with managers and operational teams.

03 · ExperimentTest, observe and adjust

Trial practices in real situations, gather feedback, examine trade-offs and refine tools through iterative learning.

04 · EmbedDevelop lasting management practices

Support adoption through training, dialogue, shared routines and clear ownership, allowing methods to evolve as the organisation learns.

Profile

VAELAN was founded in 2025 to bridge applied research and organisational transformation. Romain Vial combines fifteen years in corporate financial and operational performance management, three years as Head of Research and Foresight at CIRIDD (a French public-interest non-profit), and doctoral research in Management Sciences on Sustainable Performance Management Systems (SMPS). He also supports and trains professionals and teaches management control, sustainability performance and business model transformation in higher education. PhD defence planned early 2027.
